What does it save?

Saved

  • Each completed task’s type (lesson, review, quiz…)
  • Points and points earned
  • When it started and finished
  • The topic, its prerequisite topics, and the course
  • For each topic you have practised: Math Academy’s memory half-life and how many times it counts as practised (one request per course)
  • For each course you have studied: its topics and their prerequisites, including ones you have not started

Left out

  • Your name, email or account details
  • Your student number: used only to ask Math Academy about your own memory levels, never saved
  • Questions, answers and how long you took on each
  • Anything not listed under Saved

The extension talks only to Math Academy’s own site, from your signed-in tab, and writes the file to your computer. To make updates quick it keeps a copy of your last export in the extension’s own storage in your browser; removing the extension deletes it. Nothing is sent to the MA Field Guide.
